Send the Free Tasting Booking Link

How to trigger the automated tasting invitation email from a contact profile in GoHighLevel.

Colliers Catering
Free tasting booking
How the automation works

When the book tasting tag is applied to a contact in GoHighLevel, the system automatically sends the customer an email with the live Free Tasting booking link.

Workflow at a glance

Trigger

Add the book tasting tag to the contact profile.

Automated action

GHL sends an email titled Let's get your tasting booked in.

Customer result

The customer opens the booking page, picks their timezone, and selects an available tasting time.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Open the customer contact

    In the GoHighLevel CRM dashboard, go to Contacts. Search for the lead or customer, then click their name to open the Contact Details page.

  2. Add the tasting tag

    In the Tags panel on the left side of the contact profile, click the tag search field, type book tasting, select the matching tag, then click Apply.

  3. Confirm the email was sent

    After the tag is applied, GHL automatically sends the tasting email. Check the contact activity feed for the email titled Let's get your tasting booked in. Seeing it there confirms the booking link was sent.

  4. Know what the customer sees

    The customer clicks the link in the email and lands on the Colliers Catering - Free Tasting booking page. They choose their local timezone, pick a date, and select an available time slot between 8:00 AM and 4:30 PM.

Important: Only add the book tasting tag when the lead should receive the free tasting booking link. The email is sent automatically once the tag is applied.
