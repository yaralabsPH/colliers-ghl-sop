Manage Google review requests and replies in GHL

Understand when review emails send, reply to customer reviews, and track reputation results.

Colliers Catering
Reputation workflow
System purpose

GoHighLevel automatically requests Google reviews after qualifying first and third orders. The Reputation dashboard gives the team one place to read reviews, respond to customers, and check request performance.

Workflow at a glance

1. Order data updates

A first or latest order delivery date is populated on the contact.

2. Workflow checks

GHL waits after delivery and verifies the relevant order milestone.

3. Review email sends

An eligible customer receives a branded Google review request.

4. Team monitors reviews

Staff read and reply to reviews in Reputation and track results in Overview.

Review request automations

First order: Delivery Feedback & Review - 1st Order

LocationAutomation > Workflows > Reputation

Starts when the contact's first order delivery date field is populated. The workflow checks that the total order count is 1, waits after delivery, sends the Google review request email, and applies a tracking tag to the contact.

Third order: Delivery Feedback & Review - 3rd Order

LocationAutomation > Workflows > Reputation

Starts when the latest order delivery date field is updated. After a wait step, the workflow checks that the order count is 3 and sends the request only after the third order has been delivered.

Review request email

Find the email template

Go to Reputation > Settings > Email Requests. The template is named Review Request - First Order and uses the subject Thanks for choosing us!.

It includes Colliers Catering branding, the headline Your feedback feeds our kitchen, customer testimonials, and a Write a quick review button.

How to manage reviews

  1. Open the reviews feed

    In GHL's left sidebar, open Reputation, then select the Reviews tab.

  2. Read new customer feedback

    Review the star rating and comment for each Google review. Check for reviews that have not received a response.

  3. Reply to a review

    Click Reply on the review, write your response, then click Send to publish it.

  4. Check reputation analytics

    Go to Reputation > Overview to monitor average rating, total review count, and review trends over time.

  5. Review response and request performance

    Check unresponded reviews, automated or AI response activity, and average response time. Review request reporting includes emails sent, delivered, opened, link clicks, and conversion rates.

Note: The first- and third-order workflows use different delivery-date fields. Check the order count and delivery status before investigating a missing request. The wait duration is set inside each workflow.
