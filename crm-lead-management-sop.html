STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Manage a lead from enquiry to booking in GHL

A complete operating guide for lead entry, inquiry responses, pipeline movement, contact fields, inactive customer campaigns, and free tasting bookings.

Colliers Catering
CRM operations
Purpose

This SOP gives VAs and team members one clear reference for handling website leads in GoHighLevel, moving opportunities through the sales pipeline, and triggering the right automations at the right time.

Lead capture

Quote forms and live chat automatically create CRM profiles and team notifications.

Manual replies

Use Conversations to respond by email or SMS from the contact profile.

Pipeline tracking

Move cards through New Enquiry, Contacted, Quote Sent, Won, or Lost.

Automation triggers

Use stage changes, values, filters, and tags to trigger follow-up workflows.

1. Lead entry channels

Leads enter the CRM automatically from two main website channels.

  • Website Quote Form: Customers use the "Get My Quote" form to provide event type, location, and catering details. Submission creates a contact profile and notifies the team.
  • Website Live Chat Widget: Customers submit name, phone, email, message, and headcount. Headcount tiers trigger the correct team notification and create a CRM profile.

2. Responding to inquiries

  1. Go to Contacts and open the customer contact profile.
  2. Open the Conversations tab in the center panel.
  3. Review the interaction history before replying.
  4. Use the composer at the bottom to send a manual response.
  5. Choose Email or SMS based on the customer preference.

3. Sales pipeline management

Go to Opportunities > Sales Pipeline to track and move leads.

New EnquiryDefault stage for incoming website leads.
ContactedMove the card here after the first reply. This activates automated follow-ups if the customer stays silent.
Quote SentMove the card here after sending a quote. Enter the dollar amount in the popup Value field, such as 2000, then click Save.
WonMove the card here once the quote is accepted and payment or booking is confirmed.

Quote Sent automation timeline: Day 1 sends Email #1 and moves the card to Follow-Up. Day 3 sends an SMS. Day 8 sends Follow-Up Email #2. Day 15 sends the final SMS check-in. If there is still no response, the system adds the Lost tag and marks the opportunity as Lost.

5. Inactive customer reactivation

  1. Go to Contacts and click Filters.
  2. Search for Flex Latest Order Date [ AEST ].
  3. Set the condition to More than > 45 Days > ago, then click Apply.
  4. Select all matching contacts.
  5. Click Trigger Automation.
  6. Set an action name, such as Enroll 465 contacts to RX campaign.
  7. Select Reactivation Campaign - 45d no order.

Drip mode is critical: select Send in drip mode, use a batch quantity of 20, repeat every 5 Minutes, send Monday to Friday, and keep the sending window between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Then click Add to Automation.

6. Free tasting booking

Sending the invitation link: open the contact profile, click + under Tags, search for book-tasting, and select the tag. The system emails the customer the live calendar booking link.

Automated confirmation: once the customer books, the system emails venue and time details and tags the contact as booked-tasting.

Important: Pipeline stage changes and tags control key automations, so update them as soon as the customer status changes.
