OVERVIEW

Four steps, from message to reply

The chat widget on colliers.net.au works alongside the "Get my quote" form as a second way for people to reach out — used by both new leads and existing customers. The system handles the first two steps on its own — Lyn's job starts when the notification email lands.

STEP 1 / colliers.net.au

Lead messages the widget

Nothing for Lyn to do here

The "Have a question?" chat widget sits in the bottom-right corner of every page on colliers.net.au. Anyone can use it — a brand-new lead just browsing, or an existing customer with a quick question — and it captures name, phone, email, headcount, and their message.

STEP 2 / Automatic SMS + email

The lead gets an instant reply

Nothing for Lyn to do here

Just like the quote form, the widget sets expectations immediately — before Lyn has seen anything. A short SMS confirms the message came through, and a follow-up email adds the headcount, a phone number to call if it's urgent, and a link to the menu.

STEP 3 / Email

Lyn is notified

This is where Lyn's job starts

Which notification email Lyn gets depends on who sent the message. An existing customer gets flagged separately from a new lead, and a new lead is tiered the same way as a quote-form enquiry.

If it's an existing customer

The notification is a simple flag rather than a value tier — it just needs Lyn's attention, since this is someone who's already ordered before.

If it's a new lead

New leads are tiered High, Mid, or Low value, the same way "Get my quote" enquiries are — the subject line makes the tier clear at a glance.

What to do: click the CRM link in whichever email lands — it opens the exact contact record. For a new lead, treat the subject line as a priority signal, same as with quote-form enquiries.
STEP 4 / GoHighLevel CRM

Reply, then move the opportunity to Contacted

This is where Lyn's job starts

Same as with a quote-form enquiry: reply to the lead from inside the CRM, then update the pipeline so the board reflects that it's been actioned.

  1. Open the contact from the CRM link in the notification email.
  2. Reply using the message box at the bottom of the conversation — SMS or email, whichever fits — so the reply stays logged on the contact.
  3. Open the opportunity linked to the contact and move its stage from New Enquiry to Contacted.
  4. Do this straight after replying, not later — a handled lead left in New Enquiry looks unactioned.
Why it matters: the pipeline stage is how anyone else — including CJ — can tell at a glance whether a lead has been followed up. Leaving it in New Enquiry makes a handled lead look untouched.
